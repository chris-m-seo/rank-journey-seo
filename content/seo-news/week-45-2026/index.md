The top SEO stories and updates from week 45.

## 1. Still no core update
Google hasn’t announced a November core update as of 5 Nov. The last confirmed core updates ran 27 Mar to 8 Apr and [21 May to 2 Jun](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-may-2026-core-update-done-41435.html), and the September spam update finished on 8 Oct after nearly 14 days. Unconfirmed spikes have come and gone [since 20 Oct](https://seroundtable.com/google-search-ranking-volatility-42347.html).

**Next steps:** Annotate your analytics, and keep an eye on the [Search Status Dashboard](https://status.search.google.com/).

**Watch out for:** A core update landing during Black Friday week would be rough timing, so avoid major site changes during peak season.

## 2. AI Mode increasing capabilities
This week alone: [up to four ads per query](https://seroundtable.com/google-ai-mode-four-ads-42443.html), [bottom-of-page ads being tested](https://ppc.land/google-ai-mode-gains-a-fourth-ad-slot-below-some-answers/), [home services booking](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-ai-mode-home-services-booking-42459.html) (Angi, Thumbtack and others), [“Continue with this search”](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-ai-mode-continue-with-this-search-42475.html) and [Personal Intelligence prompts](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-personal-intelligence-popup-42468.html), and a [test of a website button on retailer product cards](https://searchenginewatch.com/google-ai-mode-tests-a-website-button-on-ecommerce-product-cards/). Google also said on its [earnings call](https://searchenginejournal.com/googles-q3-2026-earnings-call-search-ai-are-merging-into-one-unified-product/595484) that AI Overviews, AI Mode and traditional Search are becoming one product, and AI Mode ads reach the UK later this quarter.

**Next steps:** Measure visibility across AI and classic results together. Ecommerce and local-service sites should prepare for more journeys that finish inside Google.

## 3. UK: Google must report AI search data
[The CMA requires Google](https://thenextweb.com/news/uk-forces-google-to-let-publishers-opt-out-of-ai-search-results-without-losing-their-ranking) to provide AI search metrics, publisher opt-outs and better attribution, reportedly by 3 December. Google says it has nothing to announce yet. It’s unclear whether the clicks metric will be included, or who gets access.

**Next steps:** Diarise early December, and in the meantime keep your own baseline of AI referral traffic.

## 4. Brand AI Overviews moved to the right-hand column
[Spotted 2 Nov](https://seroundtable.com/google-ai-overviews-brands-right-42440.html), and still officially unannounced. It looks like a US desktop-based test for now, but it matters for brand searches. Keep an eye on how your brand queries look on desktop and mobile going forward.

## 5. Search Console and indexing
- Official Google text now says “Crawled – currently not indexed” is common and not necessarily a problem.
- Some sites show mystery click spikes (I’ve noticed the same with sites I manage), possibly from AI agents or bots, so cross-check your GA4 and server logs if this has happened to you.
- Gary Illyes says [Google can temporarily keep important URLs returning 4xx in the index](https://seroundtable.com/google-protected-important-urls-index-42464.html).
- Performance report now has [multi-select country and device filters](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-performance-report-multi-select-device-filters-42457.html).

## 6. Mixed signals on clicks
[Martin Splitt says to expect more impressions but fewer clicks from AI search](https://bsky.app/profile/rustybrick.com/post/3mzfjjnvsac22). A [Cyrus Shepard opt-out test](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-ai-mode-ctr-42421.html) (single site) suggested AI features may help brand and organic clicks. Another report showed [organic click share at a 15-month high](https://searchengineland.com/google-organic-click-share-15-month-high-report-500595), while a [110-site analysis found 3 in 4 homepages lost clicks](https://searchengineland.com/homepages-lost-organic-clicks-analysis-500246). Small samples all round, so check your own data.

## 7. AI training opt-out
OpenAI, Google, Microsoft and Anthropic [back a shared robots.txt token](https://ppc.land/google-microsoft-openai-and-anthropic-back-one-ai-training-opt-out/), `__usage_ai-training`. It covers training only, not search or grounding, and rollouts run into 2027. Decide your stance on how AI uses your content before implementing the robots.txt token.

## 8. Spam and quality
Google published [SAFE, an AI-agent spam detector](https://searchenginejournal.com/google-has-deployed-a-new-ai-spam-detector-called-safe/590918). [AI-generated Facebook pages indexed at scale](https://seroundtable.com/facebook-answers-google-42467.html) (6 million+ at the time of writing) look like a scaled content abuse target. The helpful content guidance now warns against fabricated creator profiles.

## Next steps for the week ahead

- Black Friday is 27 Nov. Verify your Merchant Center data (prices, stock, shipping, returns, images) in advance. Google also had a [Merchant Center issue on Friday](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-confirms-issues-with-merchant-center-42473.html), so it's worthwhile checking your analytics.
- Check what AI Overviews say about your brand and fix inaccuracies at the source. (Your website, social media, etc.)
- Avoid big template or migration changes from here until after Cyber Monday (30 Nov).
- [ChatGPT business analytics and citation tools](https://www.seroundtable.com/chatgpt-tools-for-business-42476.html) look likely to surface at some point, though that’s unconfirmed and OpenAI is reportedly still working on them.

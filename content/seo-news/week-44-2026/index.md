The SEO headlines for 26 October to 1 November 2026: organic ranking volatility, possibly due to an unconfirmed Google update, and the future strategy of search.

## 1. Rankings

Two unconfirmed spikes, still no named update.

Volatility spiked around [24 Oct](https://seroundtable.com/google-search-ranking-volatility-heated-42373.html) and again on [27 Oct](https://seroundtable.com/google-search-ranking-volatility-42379.html), and has been reported twice this week. The [Search Status Dashboard](https://status.search.google.com/) still shows nothing since the September spam update (24 Sept to 8 Oct, the longest this year), and the last core update was [May 2026](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-may-2026-core-update-done-41435.html).

**Next steps:** Annotate your analytics with 24 and 27 Oct, and check Search Console for real click and impression shifts by page type. Don’t change anything off the back of rank-tracker noise.

**Heading into Monday:** If Google confirms a core update, expect a rollout of a couple of weeks.

## 2. AI Mode

AI Mode is where Google’s strategy is heading, but it’s struggling to make it pay.

[Alphabet earnings](https://thenextweb.com/news/alphabet-shares-fall-google-ai-mode-search-ads) showed search growth slowing, and Google admitted AI Mode earns less than classic search ads.

In response it’s expanding [AI Mode ads (UK due in Q4), launching multi-turn ads in the US](https://letsdatascience.com/news/google-expands-ads-in-ai-mode-to-ten-more-markets-164c0dc3), and testing a blend of sponsored and organic products. It’s also testing query bubbles in AI Overviews that drop users straight into AI Mode. AI Overviews now appear on [over 80% of brand searches](https://searchenginejournal.com/ai-overviews-big-brand-searches/592280), including [82.5% of US brand searches](https://seroundtable.com/over-80-of-google-searches-have-ai-overviews-42372.html).

**Next steps:** Benchmark your AI Overview visibility and referral traffic now, and make sure your product feeds are clean if you do ecommerce.

## 3. The Click Question

New data, weak evidence.

[Cyrus Shepard’s single-site opt-out test](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-ai-mode-ctr-42421.html) put AI Overviews and AI Mode click-through at about 0.9%, but organic clicks also fell when he opted out.

A separate study reportedly found AI Mode [sends 57% of product links to Google Shopping](https://searchengineland.com/google-ai-mode-product-links-google-shopping-study-499887). [Reddit’s CEO says AI Mode “does not drive traffic.”](https://letsdatascience.com/news/reddit-ceo-says-googles-ai-mode-does-not-drive-traffic-eba69b36) All of this is small-sample, so treat it as direction, not proof.

## 4. Google Connected Apps Platform

Businesses and developers can [connect services to Search and Gemini via MCP](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/28/as-ai-agents-reshape-the-web-google-opens-gemini-and-search-to-more-apps/) (US preview, waitlist).

Another route to visibility that bypasses websites, but it’s early and the SEO impact is speculative.

## 5. Guidance and Docs

- Google’s [user-generated spam guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/monitor-debug/prevent-abuse) now covers tools that let users publish pages, content or apps.
- [Favicon docs](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/favicon-in-search): Googlebot must be able to follow redirects to the favicon page, and it must not be blocked by robots.txt.
- Mueller says [AI-search tactics like listicles will eventually be caught by Google’s systems](https://seroundtable.com/john-mueller-google-systems-ai-spam-42420.html), and told people [not to sweat the slow Search Console indexing report](https://seroundtable.com/google-downplays-importance-of-fresh-index-reporting-42381.html).
- Google updated its helpful content guidance.

## 6. Tracking and Measurement

ChatGPT’s missing UTM tags (8–15 Oct) and Gemini’s on-off UTM changes make AI referral data messy. I’ve seen my own AI-sourced revenue swing between Unassigned and the referral session source/medium channel over the past six months, which backs this up.

GA4 [reportedly missed 66% of one retailer’s holiday orders](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/ga4-missed-two-thirds-retailers-holiday-orders-check-yours-before-peak-season/593007), so test your tracking before Black Friday.

## Next steps for the week ahead

- Watch the Search Status Dashboard for a core update announcement.
- Check the data before Black Friday: GA4 tracking, product feeds, Merchant Center.
- Don’t trust one week of rank-tracker data.
- Check whether you rely on Semrush’s [Copilot tracking, which ends on 9 Nov](https://ppc.land/semrush-drops-copilot-from-ai-visibility-tracking-on-november-9/).

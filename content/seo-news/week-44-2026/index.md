The SEO headlines for 26 October to 1 November 2026: organic ranking volatility, possibly due to an unconfirmed Google update, and the future strategy of search.

## 1. Rankings

Two unconfirmed spikes, still no named update.

Volatility spiked around 24 Oct and again on 27 Oct, and has been reported twice this week. The Search Status Dashboard still shows nothing since the September spam update (24 Sept to 8 Oct, the longest this year), and the last core update was May 2026.

**Do this:** Annotate your analytics with 24 and 27 Oct, and check Search Console for real click and impression shifts by page type. Don’t change anything off the back of rank-tracker noise.

**Heading into Monday:** If Google confirms a core update, expect a rollout of a couple of weeks.

## 2. AI Mode

AI Mode is where Google’s strategy is heading, but it’s struggling to make it pay.

Alphabet earnings showed search growth slowing, and Google admitted AI Mode earns less than classic search ads.

In response it’s expanding AI Mode ads (UK due in Q4), launching multi-turn ads in the US, and testing a blend of sponsored and organic products. It’s also testing query bubbles in AI Overviews that drop users straight into AI Mode. AI Overviews now appear on over 80% of searches, including 82.5% of US brand searches.

**Do this:** Benchmark your AI Overview visibility and referral traffic now, and make sure your product feeds are clean if you do ecommerce.

## 3. The Click Question

New data, weak evidence.

Cyrus Shepard’s single-site opt-out test put AI Overviews and AI Mode click-through at about 0.9%, but organic clicks also fell when he opted out.

A separate study reportedly found AI Mode sends 57% of product links to Google Shopping. Reddit’s CEO says AI Mode “does not drive traffic.” All of this is small-sample, so treat it as direction, not proof.

## 4. Google Connected Apps Platform

Businesses and developers can connect services to Search and Gemini via MCP (US beta, waitlist).

Another route to visibility that bypasses websites, but it’s early and the SEO impact is speculative.

## 5. Guidance and Docs

- Google’s user-generated spam guidance now covers tools that let users publish pages, content or apps.
- Favicon docs: Googlebot must be able to follow redirects to the favicon page, and it must not be blocked by robots.txt.
- Mueller says AI-search tactics like listicles “will eventually be caught”, and told people not to sweat the slow Search Console indexing report.
- Google updated its helpful content guidance.

## 6. Tracking and Measurement

ChatGPT’s missing UTM tags (8–15 Oct) and Gemini’s on-off UTM changes make AI referral data messy.

GA4 reportedly missed 66% of one retailer’s holiday orders, so test your tracking before Black Friday.

## Heading into the week

- Watch the Search Status Dashboard for a core update announcement.
- Check the data before Black Friday: GA4 tracking, product feeds, Merchant Center.
- Don’t trust one week of rank-tracker data.
- Semrush stops Copilot visibility tracking on 9 Nov.
